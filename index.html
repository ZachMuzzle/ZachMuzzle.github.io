About

About me

Hello, my name is Zachary Muzzleman. I'm a software developer who loves learning new technical skills and building software that peaks my interest.

I'm currently working as an associate software developer at Fulton Bank, where I work in an agile methodology with team members to build automations and integrations based on project managers' needs. We help the bank by speeding up tasks and removing manual work — which saves the bank money and time. The majority of my work involves building, testing, and deploying APIs. We use Postman to test APIs we build or to work with vendor APIs that we integrate into our processes. I also gather data from various databases using SQL Developer (Oracle) or SQL Management (SQL Server).

I have a passion for building and learning new technologies and tech stacks that aim to help me become a more efficient developer — whether I'm working or building side projects. I'd love to break into a more traditional developer/engineering role where I use industry-standard tech stacks and build relationships with teammates who can help advance my career.
